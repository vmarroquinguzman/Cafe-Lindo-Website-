GET TO KNOW US

Rooted in Guatemala, grown with tradition.

Café Lindo is a family-owned coffee business with roots in Esquipulas, Guatemala. Growing up surrounded by coffee, it has always been a part of our lives. What started as a lifelong connection to coffee eventually inspired us to create something of our own.

Our coffee is grown on land surrounded by orange trees, banana trees, and lush greenery, creating a unique growing environment that contributes to the character of our coffee.

We take pride in producing single-origin Guatemalan coffee while supporting our local community and the people who help bring our coffee to life.

With our export license already secured, we are excited to share a little piece of Guatemala with coffee lovers beyond our borders.
