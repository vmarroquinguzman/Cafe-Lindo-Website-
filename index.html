OUR STORY

Three generations.
One passion for coffee.

Our story is deeply rooted in Esquipulas, Guatemala, where our family has grown coffee for three generations. For decades, we've passed down the knowledge, traditions, and passion for coffee farming, ensuring that our beans reflect the rich soil and culture of our homeland.

As third-generation coffee farmers, we wanted something more: a closer connection to the people who enjoy our coffee.

That's why we decided to manage the entire process, from cultivation to roasting. By staying true to our roots and keeping production local, we ensure that every cup reflects our family's craftsmanship.

Today, Café Lindo allows us to share not just our coffee, but our family's legacy directly with you.

Three generations of dedication, hard work, and pride in our heritage.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

More than coffee.
A connection.

At Café Lindo, our mission is to bring the warmth of Guatemala to your cup through exceptional, ethically sourced coffee.

From the beans we grow to the roasting process, we are committed to perfecting our craftsmanship and caring for every roast. We believe that community is at the heart of every great coffee experience.

Just like our family, we thrive on meaningful connections and shared moments. When you enjoy Café Lindo, you're not just enjoying great coffee. You're becoming part of our family.
